P1: Symlinks bypass mandatory private-source exclusions outside extras.
build_course_release.py:42-49 and :69-75
The guards check lexical names, but shutil.copy2 and shutil.copytree follow
symlinks by default. A public-looking source can therefore copy content from
a .private directory, or source/instructor material under a different name.
The output checker cannot recover that provenance once the bytes have been
copied to a normal public file, and reports no error.

Reproduced in a temporary fixture:
  .private-fixture/token.txt contains SYNTHETIC SECRET
  assets/public.txt is a symlink to that file
  copy_tree('assets', output/'assets') copies the synthetic secret
  copy_file('assets/public.txt', output/'direct.txt') copies it too
  check(root, output) returns []
No real private files were read or copied.

Recommended fix: reject symlink files, directories, and symlink ancestors
throughout publication sources, as extras already does, or validate every
resolved target and its containment before reading it. Cover direct files,
tree roots, nested files/directories, and ancestor links with tests. Do not
rely on the output checker to identify renamed private content. Reading
conversion and slide export also need source validation before rendering
when paths are configured through links.
